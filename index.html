Why it's cheaper

What “graded” means for you

  1. 1

    Same appliance

    Graded stock is usually new or ex-display. Inside, it is the same machine you would buy on the high street.

  2. 2

    Cosmetic marks only

    A small dent or scratch, often on a side that ends up hidden between units or against a wall.

  3. 3

    Much lower price

    Because it cannot be sold as perfect, you pay far less for the same performance.

Frequently asked questions

What does “graded” mean?

Graded appliances are usually new or ex-display units with minor cosmetic marks such as small dents or scratches, often from transit or showroom display. They work like new but cost less.

Where is your shop?

You can find us at 203 Strand Road, Bootle, Liverpool L20 3HJ, on Strand Road in Bootle.

Do you sell new appliances as well as graded?

Yes. We sell both brand new and graded appliances, including washing machines, tumble dryers, fridge freezers, cookers and integrated appliances.

How can I check what is in stock?

Stock changes all the time, so the quickest way is to call us on 07769 865432 or 07752 241831, or visit the store during opening hours.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We offer local delivery across Bootle, Liverpool and surrounding areas including Litherland, Crosby, Walton and Netherton. Call 07769 865432 to arrange delivery and get a price.

Can you install my new appliance and take the old one away?

Yes. We can install washing machines, dryers, fridge freezers and electric appliances on delivery and take your old appliance away. Gas cookers and hobs must be connected by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
