Buying tips

Choosing a tumble dryer

  • Heat pump

    The most energy-efficient type. Higher upfront cost but much cheaper to run.

  • Condenser

    Collects water in a tank or drains it, so no vent hose is needed. Fits almost anywhere.

  • Vented

    The cheapest option to buy, but needs a hose through a window or wall vent.

Tumble Dryers: common questions

Which type of tumble dryer is cheapest to run?

Heat pump tumble dryers use the least electricity, followed by condenser and then vented dryers.

What does “graded” mean?

Graded appliances are usually new or ex-display units with minor cosmetic marks such as small dents or scratches, often from transit or showroom display. They work like new but cost less.
