Delivery questions

Do you deliver?

Yes. We offer local delivery across Bootle, Liverpool and surrounding areas including Litherland, Crosby, Walton and Netherton. Call 07769 865432 to arrange delivery and get a price.

Can you install my new appliance and take the old one away?

Yes. We can install washing machines, dryers, fridge freezers and electric appliances on delivery and take your old appliance away. Gas cookers and hobs must be connected by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
